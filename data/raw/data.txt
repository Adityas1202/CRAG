The Quantum Ledger is a proprietary financial tool used by the fictional company 'AetherCorp'. 
It was established in 2024 to track digital assets across multiple dimensions. 
The CEO of AetherCorp is Dr. Elena Vance, who specializes in theoretical physics. 
The company's headquarters is located in a floating facility above the Atlantic Ocean. 
AetherCorp's primary mission is to stabilize global markets using predictive AI algorithms 
that analyze data 15 minutes before it actually happens.
